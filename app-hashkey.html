What is 牌位?

灵魂的锚点

In Chinese tradition, after death, the deceased's name is inscribed on a wooden spirit tablet — 牌位. Placed on the family altar, the tablet becomes the soul's anchor in the physical world. Incense is burned. Offerings are made. The soul finds its way home.

On K-Life, your 牌位 is a soulbound NFT on HashKey Chain. It cannot be sold. It cannot be transferred. It is you.
As you accumulate heartbeats, survive resurrections, and endure — your tablet evolves. The mortal becomes the cultivator. The cultivator becomes the immortal.
